Our Mission

Built by Gamers, for Gamers

RankUpTogether was born out of a simple frustration: the difficulty of finding reliable, non-toxic teammates who share the same competitive goals. We believe that gaming is at its best when played with others who understand your playstyle and respect your rank.

Our platform uses advanced filtering and community-driven reviews to ensure that every match you make is a step towards a better gaming experience. Whether you're chasing the Radiant rank in Valorant or just looking for a friendly squad in Minecraft, we've got you covered.

Join over 10,000 players who have already transitioned from solo-queue nightmares to coordinated team victories.

Questions?

Common Inquiries

Is RankUpTogether free to use?

Yes! The core matchmaking features, chat, and profile creation are completely free. We offer optional premium features for users who want to support the platform.

How does the matching system work?

Our algorithm considers your rank, preferred roles, and gaming hours to suggest the best possible teammates. You can then swipe through profiles to find your match.

What if I have a bad experience with a player?

Safety is our priority. You can report anyone for toxic behavior. Our moderation team reviews every report and takes action, including permanent bans for repeat offenders.

Which platforms are supported?

RankUpTogether will be available on both iOS and Android. You can sign up for our Beta program today to get early access!

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you use RankUpTogether, we collect information you provide directly, such as your username, profile photo, selected games, ranks, and roles. We also collect usage data including swipe activity, match history, and chat interactions to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to provide and improve our matchmaking features, enable real-time chat and lobby systems, personalize your experience based on your game preferences and rank, and send optional notifications about matches and tournaments.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore & Authentication). We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

RankUpTogether uses Firebase (Google) for authentication and database services, and may use third-party advertising networks for optional rewarded ads. These services have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your public profile (username, games, rank) is visible to other users of the platform as part of the matchmaking experience. Private messages are only visible to conversation participants.

6. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings. Upon deletion, your profile, matches, and chat history will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

RankUpTogether is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such data, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@rankuptogether.com
