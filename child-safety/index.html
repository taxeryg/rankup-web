Child Safety Standards

Last updated: June 9, 2026

At RankUpTogether, we aim to provide a safe and positive gaming experience for all our users. The safety of children and teenagers is our highest priority. In this context, we strictly apply the following standards on our platform:

1. Age Requirement and Parental Control

While our platform is open to general use, we take care to provide content that is appropriate and to match users based on their age groups. We remind you that users under the age of 13 can only use our platform under parental supervision.

2. Communication Safety and Verification

Strict rules are enforced in our in-app messaging systems to ensure the safety of our users. Sharing real-life personal information (such as real address, phone number, and identity details) is restricted by our systems, and we constantly remind our users to stay safe in this regard.

3. Experience and Reporting System

No malicious behavior such as bullying, harassment, or inappropriate language is tolerated. Our advanced reporting and complaint system, including active Admin and Moderator controls, operates round-the-clock to immediately investigate any safety violation. Suspicious accounts are permanently removed from the platform.

4. Transparency and Community Guidelines

It is mandatory for all our users to comply with our Community Guidelines. At RankUpTogether, we continuously improve our algorithms to strengthen moderation against harmful and misleading profiles, thereby creating healthy communication channels focused entirely on gaming.

5. Contact Us

If you have any concerns, complaints, or suggestions regarding child safety, please don't hesitate to contact us at any time:
